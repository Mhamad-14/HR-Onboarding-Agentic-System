# Approved Onboarding Policy (Synthetic)

This document is synthetic and exists only for the OnboardAI capstone demonstration.

## Standard timeline

Every employee receives a manager welcome meeting, an HR orientation, information-security
training, and an onboarding checklist. The coordinator should prepare these items before the
employee start date. The manager owns role-specific introductions and the first-week schedule.

## Human approval

Contract notifications, account activation, privileged access, and messages sent on behalf of
HR require approval from an authorized HR reviewer. Generated documents are drafts until that
approval is recorded. If mandatory information such as the start date or manager is missing,
the workflow must pause and request it rather than inventing a value.

## Data minimization

Only information needed for onboarding may be processed. Protected characteristics must not be
inferred or used. Full resumes, government identifiers, banking information, and medical details
must not be written to long-term conversational memory.
